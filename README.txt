Bolt 2.0

Garde Cavalry utility bot. Made by orbandit, maintained by orbandit (@just_whiz on Discord).

/background-check — Looks up a Discord user via Bloxlink, pulls their Roblox account age, current rankings in their Roblox groups, and previous usernames as well as their current Roblox Coalition/Clients & French groups via the Roblox API.
# Access is given to those with the Recruitment Team role or higher in the server

/induct — Checks the user is in the group and is at least Citoyen, runs a background check gate, assigns Discord 26eme & Cavalier rank roles, ranks the inductee to BRIGADE KELLERMAN in the Roblox group, updates nickname, strips unneeded roles, and posts the results as a formatted embed message.
# Access is given to those with the Recruitment Team role or higher in the server

/purge - Takes a Discord user, and strips them of all server-access roles, as well as de-ranking and kicking them out of the Roblox cavalry group. 
# Access is given to those with either Etat-Major permissions or Manage Roles roles or higher in the server
